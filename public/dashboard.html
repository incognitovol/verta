No pre-op baseline yet. Run a walk test before surgery and save it — every walk after surgery then shows recovery against it.
Prototype. Trunk angles, step timing, and thigh swing are real sensor data from the phones. The skeleton is a representative model driven by those signals, not full-body motion capture. Not a medical device — for demonstration and development only.

Connect the two phones

On each phone, open the camera and scan its code (or type the link). Tap Start Sensor and Allow motion access. Strap phone 1 to the lower back, phone 2 to the front of the thigh.
